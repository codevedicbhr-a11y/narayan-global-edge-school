Quality Education at the Global Edge

Narayan Global Edge School is a co-educational private school located at Patilar, Bagaha, West Champaran (PIN 845101). Established in 2016, we serve students from Class 1 to Class 8 with a focus on strong academics and values.

Our mission is to give every child access to modern learning tools, caring teachers and a safe, stimulating environment so they can grow into confident, responsible citizens.
